remember · robot memory benchmark · ongoing

Answer, look again, or abstain?

A replayable household where three people move 37 objects across two floors for three days. A patrolling dog only sees part of it. The question is when its memory is still good enough to answer.

The inspect target is scored from the agent's own evidence. Memory-reasoning errors are separated from perception errors, and structured memory is compared against last-seen images and MindJourney-generated views.

reason · Virginia Tech CS · ongoing

Tool-augmented surgical video reasoning

  • An agent that calls temporal recognition, event timelines, targeted frame inspection and instrument-focused tools before it answers.
  • A 9B VLM fine-tuned with LoRA on 2,955 tool-use trajectories, evaluated on 3,877 questions.
  • Five-fold cross-fitting plus image-removal and misalignment ablations to study tool errors and transfer across hospitals.
