Map rebuild pipeline

The official ESARBench maps are closed binaries: you can fly in them, but you cannot add a robot mesh or a collider. So I rebuilt them as editable maps: scan the packaged game, align it to public elevation data, place vegetation from satellite land cover, cook it for Unreal and export the same ground to Isaac, so the Go2 walks on what the camera sees.

Sensors and synthetic data

Every capture is pose-locked: rasterized RGB, a metric depth map, an instance mask with one color per actor, and, on the drone, a thermal image from my sensor model. They are aligned pixel for pixel. The household pipeline alone ran 8,700 snapshot-replay capture jobs. Drag across the image to compare.

benchmarks on these worlds · Virginia Tech, Xuan Wang group × SCEL

What runs on top

The worlds exist to test robot teams. Below is the scale of what runs on them; results from these studies will be posted once the papers are out.

ESARBench + Isaac physics224 episodes

All 120 official rescue tasks (4 maps, 324 victims) run with a flown drone, a policy-driven Go2 and a Husky: 110 physical and 114 teleporting-robot episodes, with every fall logged.

ranch triage benchmark509 episodes

Mass-casualty scenes with 5, 15 or 30 casualties on a 1 km² map, across 4 coordination strategies; plus 216 episodes over 8 arms on 12 thermal search scenes.

Husky + LLM planner14 missions

Yard and trail missions for a Husky A200 built from URDF, with a 16-beam lidar and RGB-D camera, run with scripted and with VLM perception.

Isaac Sim handoffs73 episodes

Rivermark testbed: 56 drone → Go2 handoff episodes over 4 layouts with crowds and decoys, plus 17 multi-casualty episodes.

A 9B VLM agent: SFT, then RL

LoRA SFT on 2,955 tool-use trajectories on long surgical video, then GRPO with dynamic sampling on up to 12 A100s stitched from preemptible Slurm jobs. 65.2 → 74.4 on 2,501 questions; the RL step alone is 69.7 → 71.1 (McNemar p = .011).

remember · robot memory benchmark

Answer, look again, or abstain?

A replayable household in which three people move 37 objects across two floors for three days. A patrolling Go2 sees only part of it; the benchmark asks when its memory is still good enough to answer.
